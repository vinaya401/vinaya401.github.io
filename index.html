About Vinayaka Gas

Vinayaka Gas is a manufacturer and supplier of high-purity, specialty and calibration gases serving industrial, laboratory and analytical applications. Headquartered in Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh, we support customers across the region with technically accurate and reliable gas solutions.

We specialize in the preparation and supply of precision gas mixtures and ultra-high purity gases used in gas chromatography, analytical instruments, environmental monitoring systems and industrial process control applications. Our operations are driven by strict quality control, consistency and safety at every stage of gas handling.

With a strong technical approach and customer-focused service, Vinayaka Gas is committed to meeting application-specific requirements and ensuring dependable performance in critical operations.

Our Products & Services

  • Calibration gas mixtures – Methane, Ethylene, Carbon Monoxide
  • Iso-Butylene calibration gases
  • Tri-gas mixtures and P-10 gas
  • Ultra-high purity gases: Nitrogen 6.0, Helium 6.0, Argon 6.0
  • Specialty, pure, refrigeration and shielding gases
  • Laboratory and analytical gas supply support
  • Industrial and process gas solutions

Quality & Safety

Quality and safety form the foundation of our operations. We follow controlled procedures to ensure accurate gas composition, consistent purity levels and safe cylinder handling.

Our quality practices help ensure reliable gas performance for critical applications such as analytical testing, research laboratories and industrial processing.
