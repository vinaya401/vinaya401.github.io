About Us

Vinayaka Gas is a manufacturer and supplier of high-purity, specialty and calibration gases for industrial, laboratory and analytical applications. We supply precision gas mixtures and ultra-high purity gases that meet strict quality, accuracy and safety requirements.

Our products are widely used in gas chromatography, analytical testing, environmental monitoring and industrial process control systems.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver technically accurate, high-purity and safe gas solutions that support critical industrial and analytical processes.

Vision: To be a trusted and reliable supplier of high-purity and calibration gases through consistent quality, technical competence and customer-focused service.

What We Provide

  • Calibration gas mixtures (Methane, Ethylene, CO, Iso-Butylene, Tri-Gas, P-10)
  • Ultra-high purity gases – Nitrogen, Helium, Argon (Grade-6)
  • Specialty, pure, refrigeration and shielding gases
  • Laboratory and analytical gas supply support
  • Safe cylinder handling and reliable delivery
