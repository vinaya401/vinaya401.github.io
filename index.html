About Us

Vinayaka Gas is a manufacturer and supplier of high-purity, specialty and calibration gases for industrial, laboratory and analytical applications. We focus on delivering reliable gas solutions that meet stringent purity, accuracy and safety requirements.

Our expertise includes precision calibration gas mixtures such as Methane, Ethylene, Carbon Monoxide, Iso-Butylene, Tri-Gas mixtures and P-10 gas, along with ultra-high purity Grade-6 gases including Nitrogen, Helium and Argon.

Vinayaka Gas supports laboratories, research institutions and industries by providing technically accurate gas solutions with consistent quality and dependable supply.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To supply high-quality, technically precise and safe gas solutions that support critical industrial and analytical processes.

Our Vision: To become a trusted supplier of high-purity and calibration gases by maintaining consistent quality, technical excellence and long-term customer relationships.
